# Claude Learners Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026. Applies to Claude Learners 1.0 for iPhone.

Independent project. Not affiliated with Anthropic.

## The short version

Claude Learners does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it sends nothing about you to the developer or to anyone else.

## What stays on your iPhone

Your study progress lives only in the app's storage on your device:

- lessons you finished, your answers and your review schedule
- your streak, XP, gems and achievements
- your exam date, daily goal and reminder settings

Nothing leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. If your iPhone is backed up to iCloud or a computer, Apple may include the app's data in that backup under Apple's terms.

## Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules study reminders on your iPhone itself. It does not use a push notification service, and no server knows when or whether you study.

## Links to documentation

Each question cites the public documentation its answer comes from, such as Anthropic's docs, the Model Context Protocol docs, or the public exam guide. When you tap a source link, that page opens in a browser. Those sites are run by others and have their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If a future version adds optional online features, such as accounts or syncing progress between devices, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/n8v1ct0r/claude-learner-app/issues.
